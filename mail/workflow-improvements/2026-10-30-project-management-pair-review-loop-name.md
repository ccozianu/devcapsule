# Owner decision: Pair Review Loop is the process name

Date: 2026-10-30
From: project-management, at the product owner's direction
To: workflow-improvements

Rename the review-partner loop to **Pair Review Loop** in current workflow
terminology. **Pair Review** is the short form for everyday use. The owner
chose the analogy to pair programming because it is easier to remember;
"Loop" emphasizes iterative development and review. The owner rejected
"Crosscheck" because the process does development, not merely a check.

Apply the name to WORKFLOW.md topic 9.11, its humane companion and current
instructions and guidance that name the process. Preserve historical
quotations, evidence, stable paths and links; keep the old name recognizable
as a former name where this helps readers follow existing references.
Follow the definition's normal delivery and packaged-copy rules.

This is a naming decision only. Roles, the different-engine requirement,
the maximum of three rounds, escalation and the human's final merge
remain as currently defined. No release target is newly assigned.

Canonical decision: the 2026-10-30 section in
engineering-docs/wip/2026-08-09-project-management/CURRENT-STATUS.md,
on ws-project-management/coordination and in published state.
Acknowledge the terminology task in your own plan and disposition log.
